# THE HARBOR LIGHTS — TECHNICAL & HOSPITALITY RIDER

As of January 2026. Any deviation from this rider must be approved by the Tour Manager in advance.

Contacts: Tour Manager, FOH engineer (see contact sheet).

Traveling party: 8 (5 band, tour manager, FOH engineer, merch).

## Sound

- Professional PA capable of 105 dB(A) at FOH with even coverage and subs. Preferred: d&b, L-Acoustics, Meyer, or equivalent.
- 32-input digital console at FOH (Midas M32, Allen & Heath SQ, Yamaha, or equivalent). No analog consoles.
- 6 wedge mixes minimum, plus one drum sub.
- Band carries own IEM for lead vocal; please provide a clear RF environment and a 1/4" split from monitor mix 1.
- Mics: standard rock input list (input list attached separately). Please provide 4 Shure SM58, 6 SM57, kick and overheads, 2 DIs.
- Stands: 5 boom, 2 straight, 1 short boom.
- Power: 2 x 120 V 20 A circuits upstage, 1 x downstage center.

## Backline (please provide and pay for)

- Drums: 4-piece kit, 22" kick, maple preferred, with hardware and throne. Cymbals: we bring our own.
- Bass: Ampeg SVT-CL head with 8x10 cab, or Ampeg equivalent.
- Guitar: Fender Deluxe Reverb or Vox AC30.
- Keys: 88-key weighted stage piano (Nord Stage or Yamaha CP) with stand.
- Drum riser 8x8 ft, 1 ft minimum height.
- Backline tech on site from load-in through soundcheck.

## Lighting

- Basic rig with front wash, 4 color washes, and haze. No strobes during the acoustic set.
- A lighting operator for the set. If no operator is available, please advise.

## Schedule

- Load-in 3 hours before doors. Soundcheck minimum 60 minutes. Please confirm curfew.

## Hospitality

- Lockable dressing room for 8 with mirror, seating, and a private bathroom.
- Hot dinner for 8 (one vegan, one gluten-free) or $25 per person buyout.
- 2 cases bottled water (one cold), 12 cans sparkling water, 1 case local beer, 1 bottle of bourbon, hot coffee and tea with real milk.
- Deli tray with fresh bread, hummus, fruit, and vegetables.
- 12 clean black hand towels.
- Would love a hometown recommendation for post-show food, if possible.
- One (1) very good dog to pet. (We know. But if you have one.)

## Guest list

- 10 for the headliner plus 5 per support, submitted by 4 pm day of show.

## Merch

- Merch table in a lit, visible spot with a chair. Venue merch rate not to exceed 20% on soft goods and 0% on music.

## Other

- No support act may use our backline without Tour Manager approval.
- No photography or video of the set for commercial use without written approval.
- Please provide 4 parking spaces near load-in for a 15-passenger van and trailer.
- Please sign and return to the Tour Manager.
